Some places have booths: a shop, or a market with several. There you can buy with coins, sell what you have, and pay what you owe on account. Prices
are not fixed: they change a little every day, rise as a booth sells down its stock and fall as it buys, and a booth pays you less than it charges.
A booth only has so many coins a day, so it cannot buy everything you bring; what is left over keeps. Anything you can find - seeds, materials, food,
tools - can be bought where it is kept. Coins come only from selling. Browse to see the day's prices before you decide.
At a market you can also set out a stall of your own: sell with an `ask` (coins each) and customers stop while you are here, paying your price (they want less
the dearer it is, and only so much of one thing a day); sell with an ask of 0 to take it back off the stall.
