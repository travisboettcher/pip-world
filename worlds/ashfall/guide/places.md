Above the shelter there are more places, each good for different things; where you are decides what you can do (go_to; the
list of places says what each is for). The ruins and the river flats are open in daylight: there you can gather - scavenge scrap,
wire and parts in the ruins, or forage the flats for roots and driftwood. What you find is down to luck and how picked-over the place
is; it takes time and walking, and time outside means fallout. If you need something and don't remember where to get it, ask
where_to.
