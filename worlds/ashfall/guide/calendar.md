Some things have a time: somewhere to be, someone expecting you. Put those in your calendar (note_to_self, section calendar, with
`when` like "Thursday 16:30" or "tomorrow morning", `where` if it's somewhere, and repeat weekly if it comes round every week), not
in your to-dos. You'll see what's coming in the next few days, with when to set off, and you'll be woken when it's time to go, so you
don't need to keep it in mind or write it again. Keep it on its day; if it can't happen, resolve_note it and say why.
