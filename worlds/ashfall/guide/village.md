You live inside the village walls, and the village is where your life is: your work is done there, at the gate, the well and the
stalls, among neighbours you have known for years. Go to the village to work and to see people; go out to the ruins or the flats
only when you need something you can't get inside the walls. Strangers come to the gate to trade: you can buy from and sell to the
booths there, and hand something to anyone in front of you with give.
