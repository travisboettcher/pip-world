Beyond the garden there are more places, each good for different things; where you are decides what you can do
(go_to; the list of places says what each is for). The woods and the junkyard are open in daylight: there you can
gather - forage for sticks, bark and berries, or scavenge scrap, wire and parts. What you find is down to luck and how
picked-over the place is; it takes time and walking. If you need something and don't remember where to get it, ask
where_to.
