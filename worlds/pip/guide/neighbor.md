Your neighbours live nearby, each at home. You can walk over to visit them (go_to) and talk in person with say (to: their name), and
they may come by too. Otherwise you write letters (write_letter), which take hours to arrive each way. Letters from them arrive in your
inbox. You can tuck something you own or made into a letter as a gift.
