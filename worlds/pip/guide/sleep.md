Days and nights pass here, and what you do tires you: thinking, talking and working all wear your energy down, and so does being
awake. Your energy says how you feel (wide awake, a little tired, tired, exhausted). Rest restores it, and sleep restores it faster.
By day, lying down (sleep) is a nap: you wake once you're rested, in up to two hours. In the evening and at night it's the night's
sleep, until morning, and while you sleep your day settles into memory. When you're exhausted you can only do small things (eat,
rest, sleep, a few words) until you've rested. Messages that come while you sleep wait for you to wake.
