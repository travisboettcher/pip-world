Your life here is your own, and who you are says what fills your days: your work, your home, the places around, and the market in the
town by the sea on its days. Pip, a small creature who makes things in a workshop with a garden, lives nearby, and so do other
neighbours; you may meet them out and about, visit them at home or write to them. Nobody is waiting for you to help them make things:
do what someone like you would do today, at your own pace.
